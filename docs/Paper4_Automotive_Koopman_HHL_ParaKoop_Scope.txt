WHAT CHANGED FROM v1 (ONE-LINE VERSION)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
v1: Validate Koopman/HHL on a self-generated cylinder wake (separate track),
    then separately train a GNN to regress Cd/Cl on DrivAerNet++ and search
    over it with a GA. The two tracks are linked by narrative ("unified claim").

v2: Make the Koopman operator the surrogate's actual mechanism — parameterized
    by structured geometry parameters theta (the same ones VICES already uses),
    calibrated jointly on AhmedML (transient, multi-geometry, real dynamics)
    and DrivAerNet++ (steady, large-scale, ground truth) — and run the whole
    learned structure BACKWARD: given a target Cd/Cl/wake behaviour and the
    physics guardrails, the system SUGGESTS novel, guaranteed-valid car body
    geometries. Geometry becomes the output, not the input. HHL solves the
    literal linear systems this operator produces, at search scale, for real
    candidate cars — not a disconnected textbook flow.

This is not a bigger version of the same plan — it is a different (stronger)
claim: not "we can evaluate designs fast," but "we can suggest designs directly."

1. ONE-LINE SUMMARY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ParaKoop learns a geometry-conditioned ("parametric") Koopman operator A(theta)
jointly from AhmedML's 500 transient Ahmed-body variants and DrivAerNet++'s
8,000 steady car shapes, exposes Cd/Cl/wake-recirculation/condition-number as
structural byproducts of that single learned linear system, solves it with HHL
at search scale, and — using the VICES/Catalan CSG design space from Paper 1 —
runs the whole differentiable chain backward to SUGGEST novel car-body
topologies directly from desired performance and physical guardrails.

2. THE CORE ARGUMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Problem (unchanged from v1 — this framing is sound):
  - Automotive aerodynamic design is constrained by parametric tools (vary
    spoiler angle, hood curvature, etc.) — same topology family every cycle
  - Each CFD evaluation: 6-48 hours on HPC clusters
  - Industry typically evaluates 10-50 designs per development cycle
  - "Topology gap": entire families of shapes are never explored because no
    tool searches topology space, only parameter space within one topology
    (this is the same argument that drove Paper 1's ALD result)

What v1 proposed, and the gap that motivated v2:
  - v1 split the work into Track A (Koopman/HHL validated on a self-generated
    cylinder wake — single geometry, transient) and Track B (CSG topology
    search driven by a directly-trained Cd/Cl regressor on DrivAerNet++).
  - The two tracks shared only a narrative "unified claim." A reviewer's
    natural question — "what does Koopman actually do for the cars you are
    optimising?" — had no mechanistic answer in v1. v1 was honest about this
    (it explicitly frames the cylinder case as validation, not mechanism).
  - Separately: v1's plan to train ONE Koopman operator on the cylinder and
    rely on it (or a learned embedding) to generalise to thousands of very
    different DrivAerNet car shapes runs into a structural problem — a
    TEMPORAL Koopman operator (z_{t+1} = A z_t) has nothing to act on for
    steady-state-only data, and asking one calibrated on a single laminar
    flow to generalise to industrial car shapes is a large, unsupported leap.

What v2 does instead (the ParaKoop architecture):
  TRACK A is removed as a separate track. There is one architecture:

  STEP 1 — Represent every car body as theta: a CSG tree over the
           Catalan-enumerated topology grammar (discrete structure) plus
           continuous CSG dimensions (lengths, angles, curvatures) — exactly
           the VICES/Track-2 representation already built and validated in
           Paper 1 (geometry/vices/, geometry/grammar.py).

  STEP 2 — Learn theta -> A(theta): a PARAMETRIC (not temporal) Koopman
           operator that is a function of these structured geometry
           parameters. Calibrate it on AhmedML's 500 transient, multi-
           geometry Ahmed-body cases (real dynamics across real geometric
           variation), and extend its fixed-point/spectral behaviour across
           DrivAerNet++'s 8,000 steady shapes (industrial scale + ground
           truth). This directly resolves the temporal/steady mismatch:
           the object being learned is the GEOMETRY-TO-OPERATOR map, not a
           single flow's time-evolution generalised by analogy.

  STEP 3 — Read performance off A(theta) as STRUCTURAL byproducts, not
           separate network heads:
             * Steady Cd, Cl, fields = fixed point of A(theta).x = b(theta)
               -> literally the linear system HHL solves (not an analogy)
             * Transient/modal behaviour (von Karman shedding, Strouhal
               numbers, bubble pumping) = eigenvalues/eigenvectors of A(theta)
             * Wake recirculation strength = a NEW metric: steady
               recirculation-bubble size (from the fixed-point field) plus
               unsteady shedding-mode energy (from A(theta)'s spectrum) —
               something no scalar Cd/Cl surrogate can expose
             * kappa(A(theta)) = condition number — does triple duty (see
               Section 5, "kappa's three roles")

  STEP 4 — Solve A(theta).x = b(theta) with HHL (Qiskit/PennyLane) for every
           shape evaluated — Ahmed cases, DrivAerNet cases, AND every novel
           candidate the search/suggestion loop produces. Report kappa,
           theoretical O(kappa^2 log N) speedup, and how kappa varies across
           shape families — a genuine, dataset-scale empirical finding.

  STEP 5 — INVERSE DESIGN (the headline capability): because
           theta -> A(theta) -> performance is a differentiable chain over a
           structured, finite, already-validated design space, run it
           BACKWARD. Specify target Cd, L/D, wake-recirculation behaviour,
           and guardrails; get suggested geometries — via a hybrid of
           grammar-conditioned generation (propose diverse, valid, novel
           topologies) and gradient-based refinement (tune continuous
           parameters precisely toward the target). See Section 5.6.

  STEP 6 — Verify: the existing GA optimiser (track2_ga_optimizer.py) checks
           and polishes the suggestion shortlist (now a 3-objective search:
           Cd, L/D, wake-recirculation strength); a small set of OpenFOAM
           ground-truth runs (~3-5 cases, mirroring Paper 1's CFD_val_rank
           validation) confirms the pipeline's predictions hold on genuinely
           novel shapes that exist in neither source dataset.

  UNIFIED CLAIM (v2, mechanistic — not narrative):
    "A single learned operator — parameterised by the same structured
    geometry representation used to search the design space — IS the
    surrogate, IS the quantum-solver's input, and IS the mechanism that lets
    us run design backward from target performance to suggested geometry.
    Koopman is not validated separately and then narratively linked to the
    rest; it is the rest."

Usability statement (kept from v1, still strong):
  "A 1% reduction in drag coefficient saves approximately $1 billion annually
  in global automotive fuel costs. ParaKoop does not just evaluate candidate
  topologies faster than classical CFD — it suggests them directly from a
  desired performance target, while guaranteeing every suggestion is
  physically valid and constructible."

3. DATASETS — TWO REAL PUBLIC DATASETS + GENERATED NOVEL TOPOLOGIES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
v1 proposed generating a small (~10-20 case) bridging transient dataset because
DrivAerNet++ alone cannot teach a model how geometry maps to DYNAMICS (it has
no time series). v2 replaces that self-generation plan with a dataset that
already exists, is free, and is two orders of magnitude larger:

DATASET A — AhmedML (replaces v1's self-generated cylinder wake + bridging set)
  Source:      Ashton et al. 2024, arXiv:2407.20801, CC-BY-SA (free download)
               Paper:        https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.20801
               Dataset site: https://neilashton.github.io/caemldatasets/ahmedml/
  Content:     500 geometric variants of the Ahmed body, each run TRANSIENTLY
               with a high-fidelity hybrid RANS-LES approach in OpenFOAM,
               ~80 convective time units per case, ~20M-cell meshes
  Why it matters: this is the multi-geometry + time-resolved combination that
               makes learning theta -> A(theta) possible at all. A single
               cylinder case (v1's plan) is structurally incapable of teaching
               geometry-conditioning — you cannot learn how an operator varies
               with geometry by observing only one geometry. AhmedML gives
               500 (geometry, dynamics) pairs, ready-made, for free.
  Role:        Calibrates theta -> A(theta) with REAL dynamics; validates
               extracted modal frequencies/Strouhal numbers against the
               dataset's own published flow physics and the wider Ahmed-body
               DMD literature (bubble pumping St ~ 0.07, shedding St ~ 0.13-0.17)

DATASET B — DrivAerNet++ (kept from v1, role sharpened)
  Institution: MIT; Paper: NeurIPS 2024 (arXiv 2406.09624)
  Size:        8,000 car body geometries + RANS CFD (~39 TB total)
  Flow type:   Steady-state RANS, k-omega SST, Re ~ 3-5 million
  Fields:      3D velocity, pressure, wall shear stress, Cd, Cl
  Role:        Supplies geometric SCALE and DIVERSITY (full production car
               families — fastback/notchback/estateback) that AhmedML's 500
               Ahmed-family shapes cannot; anchors the steady/fixed-point
               side of theta -> A(theta) against industrial-scale ground truth;
               supplies the topology-search baseline to beat
  NOT suitable for: learning dynamics (no time series — confirmed, as in v1)
  IS suitable for:  scale, diversity, Cd/Cl ground truth, baseline benchmark

HOW THE TWO COMBINE (the methodological crux of v2):
  AhmedML teaches the model HOW GEOMETRY DETERMINES DYNAMICS (many geometries
  x time series, but one shape family). DrivAerNet++ teaches the model HOW
  GEOMETRY DETERMINES STEADY PERFORMANCE at industrial scale and diversity
  (many more, more realistic geometries, but only steady snapshots). Training
  one parametric operator A(theta) jointly across both fuses dynamics-richness
  with geometry-richness — to our knowledge a new way to combine exactly these
  two dataset TYPES, turning what looks like "two datasets that don't fit
  together" into complementary halves of one training signal.

DATASET C — Novel topologies (generated via VICES/Catalan, not downloaded)
  Source:      Track 2's CSG/SDF engine from Paper 1 (geometry/vices/:
               csg.py, primitives.py, variants.py, voxelizer.py; topology
               grammar in geometry/grammar.py), extended with automotive
               primitives (CarBody, Spoiler, Diffuser, WheelArch)
  Role:        Defines the candidate space the inverse-design engine searches
               and the GA verifies — shapes in NEITHER AhmedML nor
               DrivAerNet++. This is where "leverage the previous geometry
               work" lands centrally, not as an afterthought: VICES/Catalan
               is simultaneously (a) the structured parameter space theta
               that A is conditioned on, and (b) the guaranteed-valid OUTPUT
               representation the inverse model decodes into (see Sec 5.6)

OPTIONAL — Cylinder wake Re=100 (kept from v1 as a cheap sanity check only)
  Cheap (30-60 min), textbook EDMD validation that the machinery extracts the
  expected unit-circle eigenvalues before trusting it on Ahmed/DrivAer shapes.
  Arguably unnecessary now that AhmedML covers the same need at vastly greater
  scale and realism — keep only if you want an extra "reviewers will recognise
  this" anchor.

4. WHY THIS DATASET STRATEGY IS STRONGER THAN v1's
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
v1's two-dataset structure was honest and well-reasoned, but the cylinder-wake
half was: (a) self-generated (cost, time, infrastructure burden), (b) a SINGLE
geometry (cannot teach geometry-conditioning, by construction), and (c) only
narratively connected to the DrivAerNet half.

v2's structure replaces all three weaknesses with strengths:
  (a) AhmedML is free, public, and already exists — zero generation cost/risk
  (b) 500 distinct geometries x full transient time series — exactly the
      training signal a geometry-CONDITIONED operator requires
  (c) AhmedML and DrivAerNet++ are fused into ONE operator, not two narratively
      linked tracks — the connection is mechanistic (same A, same theta, same
      fixed-point/spectral readout) not rhetorical

  The unified narrative becomes literal instead of aspirational:
    "We learn one geometry-conditioned linear operator from real transient
    data on 500 shapes and real steady data on 8,000 shapes; every quantity
    we report — Cd, Cl, wake structure, quantum-solvability, and the
    suggested designs themselves — is a structural readout of that one object."

5. TECHNICAL COMPONENTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

5.1 Structured Geometry Representation — theta (REUSE — geometry/vices/, grammar.py)
  - CSG tree over Catalan-enumerated topology grammar (discrete: which
    primitives, which boolean ops, what arrangement) + continuous CSG
    dimensions (lengths, angles, curvatures)
  - Automotive primitives (NEW, small — 2-3 days, as v1 scoped):
      CarBody(length, width, height, roof_curve), Spoiler(height, angle),
      Diffuser(angle, length), WheelArch(x, y, radius)
  - This single representation theta is (a) what A is conditioned on, AND
    (b) the guaranteed-valid output space the inverse model decodes into —
    serving double duty (see 5.6)

5.2 Parametric Koopman Calibration on AhmedML (NEW — the architectural core)
  - Encoder: MultiHeadMGN backbone (reused from Paper 1 / app.py) maps
    theta -> latent geometry representation
  - Koopman head: learned map theta -> (A(theta), b(theta), lifting functions
    phi), trained on AhmedML's 500 cases via a fixed-point loss:
    A(theta).phi(x_mean) ~= phi(x_mean) + b(theta) in the lifted observable
    space, where phi(x_mean) is the sampled mean-flow field at fixed probe
    locations (3,456 probes × 4 fields = 13,824-dim observable vector)
  - Library: PyDMD-compatible formulation (as v1 scoped for its EDMD step)

  DATA FORMAT CLARIFICATION (confirmed during development):
    AhmedML stores TIME-AVERAGED mean fields (UMean, pMean, vorticityMean)
    averaged over ~80 convective time units of hybrid RANS-LES. Individual
    time snapshots are NOT stored in the public dataset (too large). This
    means the 500 runs provide 500 (theta, phi_mean) pairs — geometry to
    mean-flow-observable — not a time series for any single geometry.
    This is fully compatible with the PARAMETRIC Koopman formulation
    (learning theta → A(theta) across geometries) but means the training
    loss is a fixed-point/steady-state residual, not a temporal EDMD step.

  STROUHAL VALIDATION — METHOD AND CAVEAT (important for paper writing):
    Because AhmedML does not provide individual time snapshots, Strouhal
    numbers CANNOT be extracted via temporal FFT of the simulation data.
    Validation is performed by TWO complementary methods instead:

    Method A — Operator eigenvalue spectrum vs. published literature:
      Once A(theta) is trained, its eigenvalues lambda_k are mapped to
      non-dimensional frequencies via St = Im(lambda_k)*L / (2*pi*U_inf).
      The resulting St values are compared against well-documented Ahmed-body
      experimental and DNS literature: bubble pumping St ~ 0.07, vortex
      shedding St ~ 0.13-0.17 (Grandemange et al. 2013, Evstafyeva et al.
      2017). This validates that the learned operator's SPECTRAL STRUCTURE
      captures real flow physics, without requiring time-series data from
      this specific dataset.

    Method B — Spatial vorticity patterns:
      AhmedML's vorticityMean field contains the spatial signature of von
      Karman structures (recirculation zone shape, vortex-core positions).
      The Koopman operator's eigenvectors are compared against these spatial
      patterns to confirm the learned modes correspond to known physical
      structures (wake bubble, A-pillar vortices, trailing vortex pairs).

    State explicitly in the paper (Methods §3.4 / Limitations):
      "Strouhal validation uses eigenvalue-spectrum comparison against
      published Ahmed-body literature rather than temporal-FFT extraction
      from the training dataset, as AhmedML provides time-mean fields.
      This is consistent with the parametric (not temporal) Koopman
      formulation adopted here, and does not affect the HHL validation
      dependency (§5.5) — see Design-Rationale Q9."

  - Effort: ~1.5-2 weeks (replaces v1's separate "Koopman/EDMD on cylinder"
    line item — same library, much richer calibration data)

5.3 Steady-State Extension via DrivAerNet++ (NEW — completes the fusion)
  - The SAME theta -> A(theta) map (now correctly understood as parametric,
    not a temporally-generalised operator) is evaluated at DrivAerNet++'s
    8,000 theta points
  - Steady Cd, Cl, fields = fixed point of A(theta).x = b(theta), supervised
    against DrivAerNet++ ground truth
  - Target: OOF rho > 0.85 (Paper 1's reference benchmark used 123 shapes;
    8,000 should clear this if the fusion works — v1's open question Q3)
  - Effort: ~3-4 days on top of 5.2 (shares the same architecture/training
    loop; this is "extend," not "retrain from scratch")

5.4 Derived Metrics (NEW — structural byproducts of A(theta))
  - Cd, Cl, L/D — from the fixed point
  - WAKE RECIRCULATION STRENGTH — steady component (recirculation-bubble
    size/strength from the fixed-point field x) + unsteady component
    (shedding-mode energy/amplitude from A(theta)'s spectrum). Connects to:
    base-pressure drag, EV battery thermal/cooling management (hot
    recirculated air re-ingestion), cabin acoustics/buffeting, rear-end
    soiling — a richer business story than Cd alone, and a quantity NO
    scalar Cd/Cl surrogate can produce
  - kappa(A(theta)) — condition number. THREE ROLES in one diagnostic:
      (i)   flow-regime characterisation (laminar/simple -> low kappa;
            complex/turbulent -> higher kappa)
      (ii)  quantum-solvability / HHL speedup potential (kappa ~ 10-100
            -> HHL shines; kappa >> 1000 -> advantage diminishes, as v1
            already noted for turbulent flows)
      (iii) NEWLY RECOGNISED: trustworthiness of gradient-based design
            refinement (well-conditioned A means both "HHL helps" and "the
            local performance landscape is smooth enough to trust gradient
            descent" — see 5.6). One number, three engineering meanings —
            a genuinely elegant unification worth foregrounding in the paper

5.5 HHL Quantum Linear Solving (REUSE plan from v1, scope expanded)
  - Library: Qiskit or PennyLane, run on the LITERAL A(theta).x = b(theta)
    that the surrogate produces — for AhmedML cases, DrivAerNet cases, AND
    every candidate the inverse-design loop generates (Section 5.6)
  - Report: kappa(A(theta)) distributions BY SHAPE FAMILY (Ahmed vs. DrivAer
    vs. novel suggestions), theoretical O(kappa^2 log N) speedup
  - Honest caveat (kept from v1): classical simulation of HHL cannot
    demonstrate actual speedup — report theoretically via kappa and N analysis
  - This is what makes the Koopman -> HHL connection MECHANISTIC rather than
    illustrative: HHL runs on real systems for real cars, at search scale —
    not once, on one disconnected textbook flow
  - VALIDATION DEPENDENCY (important — states explicitly what 5.2's Strouhal
    check is FOR): kappa(A(theta)) and the theoretical speedup are only
    meaningful to the extent A(theta) is itself a physically faithful
    operator. That faithfulness is exactly what Section 5.2's Strouhal-number
    validation establishes (extracted modal frequencies vs. literature). The
    two validations are deliberately kept SEPARATE and serve different
    purposes — Strouhal validation certifies the INPUT to HHL is physically
    meaningful (a spectral/modal correctness check on A(theta) itself); kappa/
    speedup analysis characterises HHL's BEHAVIOUR on that input (a numerical-
    conditioning / quantum-readiness check). Put together: Strouhal validation
    earns the right to trust the HHL results; kappa/speedup analysis is what
    you report once that trust is earned. State this dependency explicitly in
    the paper (Section 4.2/4.3) — it pre-empts the reviewer question "how do
    you know the systems HHL is solving aren't garbage?" with a direct answer,
    and it is what makes the Koopman -> HHL link auditable rather than assumed.

5.6 Inverse Design — the Hybrid Mechanism (NEW — the headline capability)
  Rather than a forward surrogate plus a GA that blindly samples and scores,
  run theta -> A(theta) -> performance BACKWARD: specify targets, get
  suggested geometries. Two-stage hybrid (chosen over pure-gradient or
  pure-generative alternatives — see the companion Design-Rationale doc for
  the full comparison):

  STAGE A — Grammar-conditioned generation (handles DISCRETE structure)
    - A generative model over the Catalan CSG-tree grammar, conditioned on
      target performance (Cd, L/D, wake-recirculation strength) and guardrail
      bounds, proposes diverse candidate topologies
    - Grammar-constrained decoding GUARANTEES every sample is a syntactically
      valid, constructible CSG tree (cannot produce nonsense geometry)
    - NOVELTY TERM: reward topologies with large Catalan-grammar edit-distance
      from anything in AhmedML/DrivAerNet++ (subject to guardrail
      satisfaction) — operationalises "out-of-the-box," directly extending
      Paper 1's "topology gap" argument into generation itself
    - No new inverse-labelled data needed: trains on the SAME (geometry,
      performance) pairs from AhmedML/DrivAerNet++/GA-search runs, just
      conditioned in the reverse direction (standard conditional-generative
      practice)

  STAGE B — Gradient-based refinement (handles CONTINUOUS parameters)
    - For each proposed topology (now a FIXED discrete structure), run
      latent/test-time optimisation on the continuous CSG parameters:
      descend on || predicted_performance(theta) - target || + guardrail
      penalty, where predicted_performance is read off the differentiable
      theta -> A(theta) chain
    - This is where kappa(A(theta))'s third role (5.4.iii) matters: a
      well-conditioned operator means the gradient signal can be trusted

  GUARDRAIL ENFORCEMENT IS STRUCTURAL, NOT STATISTICAL
    - Grammar-constrained decoding -> guaranteed syntactic validity
    - Physical guardrails (Re, Ma, Eu ranges; Cd_range; L/D_range) -> hard
      post-hoc check with REJECTION AND RESAMPLING for violations (not a
      soft penalty a model could learn to ignore at the tails)

  WHY THIS HYBRID (brief — full reasoning in the Design-Rationale doc):
    pure gradient-based struggles with inherently discrete topology choice
    (needs fragile relaxations) and tends toward local refinement (working
    against "out-of-the-box"); pure grammar-generative is valid and diverse
    but leaves the differentiable Koopman chain's gradient information
    unused. The hybrid gets validity+diversity from the grammar side
    (which you've already built and validated in Paper 1) and precision
    from the gradient side (which the new architecture uniquely provides).

5.7 Automotive VICES Geometry (REUSE + extend — 2-3 days, as v1 scoped)
  - Reuse geometry/vices/ wholesale; add the four automotive primitives
    listed in 5.1; Catalan characterisation carried over unchanged
  - Now serves DOUBLE DUTY: structured conditioning space for A(theta) AND
    guaranteed-valid output representation for inverse design (5.6)

5.8 GA Optimiser — Now a Verifier/Refiner, Not the Primary Search (REUSE,
    fitness expanded)
  - Reuse optimization/track2_ga_optimizer.py largely unchanged
  - Fitness EXPANDS from 2 objectives (Cd, L/D — v1's plan) to 3: add wake-
    recirculation strength — a genuine multi-objective search over a
    dimension NO existing automotive optimiser can even compute
  - Role shifts from "primary search mechanism" (v1) to "verifier/refiner
    around what the inverse model proposes" (v2) — checks and polishes the
    suggestion shortlist before the small OpenFOAM validation round

5.9 Physics Guardrails — Lighter Set Than ALD (REUSE + adapt — half day,
    EXACTLY as v1 scoped in its section 5.7 — confirmed correct, unchanged)
  - Keep only Re, Ma, Eu (3 flow-regime dimensionless numbers — Reynolds,
    Mach, Euler — vs. ALD's full 11-group set)
  - Add automotive output-range checks: Cd_range [0.1, 0.5], L/D_range [0-5]
  - DROP all 8 ALD-specific chemical/thermal/species guardrails (Da, Sc, Pr,
    Nu, Bi, Sh, Pe_h, Pe_m) — automotive aerodynamics has no reaction/
    species/thermal regime to police
  - Adapt physics/guardrails.py (GuardrailBounds) — minor, half-day change
  - Enforced STRUCTURALLY in the inverse-design loop (5.6: hard check +
    reject/resample), not merely as a soft training-time penalty

5.10 OpenFOAM — Role Shifted from Generation to Validation-Only
  v1 used OpenFOAM to GENERATE training data (cylinder wake + Docker
  pipeline reuse). Because AhmedML and DrivAerNet++ now supply the
  transient and steady training data respectively, OpenFOAM's footprint
  shrinks substantially:
    - ~3-5 ground-truth runs (mirroring Paper 1's CFD_val_rank{1,2,3} cases)
      on the TOP novel suggested-and-refined topologies — geometries that
      exist in NEITHER source dataset — confirming the pipeline's
      predictions hold on genuinely new shapes, not just interpolations
    - Optionally, the cheap cylinder-wake sanity case (Section 3, Dataset
      "Optional")
  This is a FEATURE, not a gap: a much smaller CFD infrastructure burden
  than v1's plan, freeing effort for the architecture and inverse-design work.

8. NOVELTY CLAIMS (SHARPENED FROM v1)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Primary:
  1. First PARAMETRIC (geometry-conditioned, not temporally-generalised)
     Koopman operator for aerodynamic surrogate modelling, calibrated jointly
     across a transient multi-geometry dataset (AhmedML) and a large
     steady-state dataset (DrivAerNet++) — a new way to fuse exactly these
     two data types
  2. First MECHANISTICALLY-INTEGRATED Koopman -> HHL pipeline at dataset
     scale: the linear systems HHL solves are the literal outputs of the
     surrogate for real candidate geometries — not an illustrative
     side-study on a disconnected canonical flow (v1's structure)
  3. First INVERSE-DESIGN ENGINE for automotive aerodynamics that outputs
     geometry directly from target performance — a hybrid grammar-
     conditioned-generation + gradient-refinement mechanism over a
     Catalan/CSG space, with structurally-guaranteed validity and guardrail
     compliance (vs. the field's standard forward-surrogate-plus-search loop)
  4. Formal chain, now OPERATIONALISED rather than asserted: chaos theory ->
     Koopman linearisation -> quantum linear solving -> differentiable,
     invertible geometric design — a complete theoretical-to-practical chain

Secondary:
  5. WAKE RECIRCULATION STRENGTH as a first-class design objective — derivable
     only from a model that predicts full transient wake fields and their
     modal decomposition; connects to base-pressure drag, EV thermal
     management, cabin acoustics, and soiling — dimensions no scalar Cd/Cl
     optimiser can evaluate
  6. kappa(A(theta)) as a UNIFYING DIAGNOSTIC — flow regime, quantum-
     solvability, AND gradient-refinement trustworthiness in one number;
     opens a new research question (how does topology affect Koopman-
     operator conditioning, and hence both quantum-readiness and
     design-search reliability?)
  7. Cross-domain transfer of VICES + MultiHeadMGN + Catalan formalism
     (ALD -> automotive), with the geometry-synthesis infrastructure now
     serving DOUBLE DUTY as both output representation and conditioning
     structure of the inverse-design mechanism
  8. Bridges QuantumRAG (quantum Hilbert space) + Paper 1 (CSG CFD) — kept
     from v1's framing, now strengthened: Koopman lifts automotive CFD into
     Hilbert space, HHL solves it, and the SAME structure runs backward to
     suggest designs — the same quantum linear algebra as QuantumRAG, now
     doing double duty as a generative mechanism

What this is NOT (kept from v1 — still the right positioning):
  - NOT a PINN paper
  - NOT a DrivAerNet accuracy improvement
  - NOT a quantum hardware paper (HHL simulated classically)
  - NOT building a new CFD solver
